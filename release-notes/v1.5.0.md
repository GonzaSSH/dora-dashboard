A clearer front page.

- **A banner at the top:** "Performance metrics for engineering teams", with one sentence on
  what the app does and the repository field right under it.
- **Straight to the result:** after you click **Analyse** or pick a saved example, the page
  scrolls down to the result (instantly if you've asked your device for reduced motion).
- **A small chart on every metric card:** one bar per week under the number, so you can see
  at a glance how things moved over the period. Red marks releases that needed an urgent fix,
  and a grey line shows the previous 90 days where it applies. Hover or tap a bar for that week.
- **Everything lines up:** numbers, charts and sentences sit at the same height across the cards.
- **Clearer buttons:** "Why a token?", "Settings", "How this was worked out" and the release list
  now look like buttons, with an icon and an arrow that flips when open.
- **"Medium"** replaces "In between" in the rating.
- **Start over in one click:** "Analyse a repository…" in the sidebar now opens a clean start
  page, with no repository, settings or corrections carried over.
- **Fix:** changing a correction in the releases list no longer throws the page back up or
  loses keyboard focus; you stay on the release you just corrected.
