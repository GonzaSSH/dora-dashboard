A new look: dark first, bold and easier to scan. The numbers, settings, links and wording
are unchanged.

**Design**
- Dark theme by default, with a light theme that follows your system setting.
- Bold type (Manrope, self-hosted, 25 KB), big metric numbers, rounded surfaces separated
  by tone instead of lines, and one accent colour of our own, "signal amber".
- Results read like a playlist page: the repository name large on a soft gradient, the
  period and the definitions used beneath it, and **Share** and **Analyse live** buttons.
- The releases list is a track list: one numbered row per release with its date, lead
  time and status tags ("Failed", "Rework", "manually corrected").
- Skeleton cards while an analysis loads.

**Navigation**
- A sidebar with the saved examples and Settings; on phones it folds into a top bar with
  a menu.
- A third saved example: this dashboard, measured on its own releases.
- **Share** copies the page address, which already holds your settings and corrections
  (never your token).

**Accessibility**
- All text meets WCAG AA contrast in both themes, measured in the browser by the tests.
- Visible keyboard focus everywhere, controls at least 44 px tall on phones, status never
  shown by colour alone, and no animation for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
